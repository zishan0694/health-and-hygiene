Personal Hygiene Standards

WHO 6-Step Handwashing

Wet hands with clean water, apply soap, rub palm to palm, lather back of hands, interlace fingers, rub thumb rotationaly, rinse well, and dry with a clean towel.

Oral & Dental Care

Brush twice daily for 2 minutes using fluoride toothpaste. Replace toothbrush every 3 months and clean tongue daily.
