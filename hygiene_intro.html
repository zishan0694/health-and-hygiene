Introduction to Hygiene

Hygiene refers to conditions and practices that help to maintain health and prevent the spread of diseases. Modern hygiene practices encompass personal cleanlines, environmental sanitation, safe food handling, and respiratory care.

WHAT TO DO (Do's)

  • Wash hands with soap and water for at least 20 seconds before eating and after using the restroom.
  • Brush teeth twice daily and floss regularly to prevent oral infections and plaque buildup.
  • Cover your mouth and nose with a tissue or elbow when coughing or sneezing.
  • Keep fingernails clean, short, and trimmed regularly to prevent pathogen accumulation.
  • Keep living areas clean, ventilated, and regularly disinfected.

WHAT NOT TO DO (Don'ts)

  • Do NOT touch your eyes, nose, or mouth with unwashed hands.
  • Do NOT share personal items like towels, razors, toothbrushes, or comb with others.
  • Do NOT wear damp or unwashed clothes and socks for extended durations.
  • Do NOT consume uncovered street food or untreated water from unknown sources.
  • Do NOT ignore minor skin cuts or open wounds without proper cleansing and bandaging.
